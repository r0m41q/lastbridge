Paste a YouTube or Last.fm playlist URL, or a Last.fm playlist ID such as 12354876. Last.fm imports use your linked account and stay private. Set their refresh interval in days on the import report; default and minimum: 1 day. Only playlists readable under your Last.fm username can be imported.

About downloads and storage

Ordinary playlists store references only. Keep downloaded queues tracks for permanent server storage, including later additions. This does not download anything onto your phone or add hearts. Download progress and retry are on the Saved library page.

Releasing a playlist keeps its track list. Audio stays retained while any favorite or downloaded playlist needs it; otherwise it returns to the temporary cache. Loaded client queues are unchanged.
